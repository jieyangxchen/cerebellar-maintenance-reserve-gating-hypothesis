# Research status and closure note

**Status date: 7 September 2026. Research and further submissions are paused.**

This repository is being retained as a public, read-only research archive. It contains an unvalidated hypothesis, illustrative models, and research-planning concepts. It is not a peer-reviewed publication, a demonstrated disease mechanism, an active clinical study, or a treatment recommendation.

## Submission history

- **The Cerebellum:** the initial manuscript was submitted on 21 August 2026; a figure-citation technical correction was resubmitted on 24 August 2026. The submission was subsequently rejected. The editor described the manuscript as speculative and invited a new submission after real-world validation. That submission is closed.
- **Medical Hypotheses:** the tailored manuscript was submitted on 1 September 2026 and rejected on 4 September 2026. That submission is closed.

These outcomes are recorded from the author's submission-system records and decision letters. The private correspondence, screenshots, and account details are not reproduced here. The outcomes are not evidence that the hypothesis is either correct or false, and they do not establish a scientific endorsement. Neither submission resulted in a peer-reviewed publication.

## Why the work is paused

A follow-up author-side mathematical and originality assessment did not establish a sufficiently distinct, empirically supported additional contribution to justify continuing the present submission route. Relevant prior work includes:

- **Kundra et al. (2020), “A Cell- and Tissue-Specific Weakness of the Protein Homeostasis System Underlies Brain Vulnerability to Protein Aggregation.”** This study relates aggregation vulnerability to the protein-homeostasis network and a protection measure. Broad claims to have first introduced a pressure–protection balance would therefore be inappropriate. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2020.100934)
- **Jiang et al. (2025), “Early proteasome downregulation and dysfunction drive proteostasis failure in Alzheimer's disease.”** Its NFE2L1 resist score uses predicted minus observed proteasome expression. This is direct prior art for a broad residual-based proteostasis-response measure, although it is not asserted to be identical to every proposed SCA6-specific design. [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaf222)

These examples are not an exhaustive novelty review. They do not show that every possible disease-specific extension has already been studied. Equally, developing an idea independently, changing a score name, or applying a known method to a different disease does not by itself establish a new scientific contribution.

The candidate expression programs considered in follow-up feasibility work have not been validated as independent measures of pathological burden or functional maintenance capacity. No completed independent empirical validation is claimed for the unknown input, gate, outward loss, or reserve mechanism. Expression coverage, illustrative simulations, and software checks are not substitutes for such validation.

Pausing this work is a resource and research-direction decision. It is **not** a claim of empirical falsification, a misconduct finding, or a retraction of a published paper.
