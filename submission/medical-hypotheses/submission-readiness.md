# Version 0.3.1 submission-readiness record

## Editorial positioning

The revised article is positioned as a hypothesis rather than as a review claiming an established environmental mechanism. It states one central proposition, distinguishes an observable association layer from an optional mechanistic layer, and specifies what would reject each layer. The editorial rejection of version 0.2.3 by *The Cerebellum* is treated as informative: the current package does not imply that real-world validation already exists.

## Evidence-bounded review

- Established cerebellar-reserve concepts are retained as prior art rather than relabelled as a novel reserve discovery.
- Human onset heterogeneity, rural-environment association, preataxic biomarkers, animal circuit studies, and known-target intervention examples remain bounded to their cited populations and models.
- A non-linear statistical association is not equated with the proposed gate.
- The unknown input, gate, reserve flux, and four-zone curve remain explicitly unobserved.
- Alternative explanations include exposure error, selection, survivor bias, reverse causation, competing risks, subgroup mixtures, and unmeasured confounding.
- The clinical discussion is restricted to known biological targets and prospective research gates.

This is an internal working assessment, not specialist endorsement. Before any study is implemented, the protocol and analysis plan require independent review by an ataxia clinician, biostatistician, environmental epidemiologist, ethics body, sponsor, and relevant regulators.

## Scientific-language review

The English manuscript received five focused passes for argument order, paragraph focus, sentence clarity, terminology consistency, and final copy-editing. The final source uses `preataxic`, `phenoconversion`, `non-linear`, `outward loss`, and `Fig.` consistently where those concepts apply. The prose avoids treating $X$, $R$, $B$, $P$, or $L$ as discovered substances or validated clinical measures.

## Package decision

The upload package contains a scrubbed, anonymous reviewer manuscript plus separate title page, cover letter, Highlights, CRediT author statement, completed Elsevier DOCI form, and Ethics statement files. The longer cohort, statistical-analysis, and early-intervention documents remain linked in the public repository so that readers can inspect the executable research plan without presenting it as completed real-world validation.

## Double-anonymized review boundary

The reviewer manuscript removes direct author identifiers, the direct repository URL, acknowledgements, and named contributor attribution. The six repository-generated Word files have personal metadata and revision-session identifiers scrubbed. The publisher-generated DOCI form is retained byte-for-byte and has blank creator and last-modified-by core properties. Because the public preprint can be found by searching the exact manuscript title, this package cannot guarantee reviewer anonymity; it implements the file-level separation required for double-anonymized review and discloses the public preprint to the editorial office.
