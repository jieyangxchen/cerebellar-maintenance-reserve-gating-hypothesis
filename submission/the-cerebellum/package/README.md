**Package status:** locally prepared from manuscript version 0.2.1 while the presubmission inquiry submitted on 19 August 2026 is awaiting an editorial reply. These files have not been formally submitted to the journal.

## Word files

- `01_Main_Manuscript.docx` — complete English manuscript with title-page information, abstract, six keywords, editable Word-equation objects, three original figures, declarations, and references.
- `02_Title_Page.docx` — separate title page for a portal request or editorial-office workflow.
- `03_Cover_Letter.docx` — journal-specific cover letter. Its article-category sentence must be checked against the presubmission reply before formal submission.
- `04_Supplementary_Protocols.docx` — proposed SCA3-led cohort, statistical-analysis plan, and genotype-specific early-intervention concepts. It is a research-planning supplement, not a clinical protocol ready for implementation.

All Word files are generated by `../build_submission_package.py`; the Markdown manuscript and protocol files remain the canonical sources.

The four Word outputs pass deterministic SHA-256 rebuild, accessibility, heading, section, equation, and inline-image audits. Rendered QA pages are temporary review artifacts and are not submission files.

## Safety and evidence boundary

The article is a hypothesis and research framework. It reports no participant-level data and does not authorize exposure escalation, unknown-input treatment, neural injury, medication, stimulation, gene intervention, or clinical implementation of the supporting protocol concepts.
