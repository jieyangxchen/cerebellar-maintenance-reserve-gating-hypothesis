# Changelog

All notable public manuscript-package changes are recorded here. Versions describe repository research artifacts, not journal publication status.

## 0.2.1 — 19 August 2026

- Added the author's ORCID and confirmed public affiliation.
- Replaced provisional funding and competing-interest language with author-confirmed declarations.
- Finalized the author-contribution and AI-assistance statements for the current sole-author version.
- Refreshed the evidence cut-off, rechecked NCT05822908, and added the 2026 Ataxia Global Initiative MRI endpoint consensus.
- Updated the presubmission inquiry and *The Cerebellum* checklist to reflect the completed public metadata.

## 0.2.0 — 18 August 2026

- Retitled the article as a maintenance–reserve–gating framework without implying that an external factor has been discovered.
- Nested dynamic reserve within established structural and functional cerebellar-reserve theory.
- Separated the observable environmental model from the hypothetical $X$/gating mechanism.
- Added explicit reserve-to-phenoconversion observation models and layer-specific falsification rules.
- Condensed cohort and early-intervention detail in the manuscript while retaining full protocol and SAP files in the repository.
- Tailored the package for a presubmission inquiry to *The Cerebellum*.

## 0.1.0 — 7 August 2026

- Published the initial hypothesis manuscript, figures, evidence audit, prospective cohort protocol, statistical analysis plan, and early-intervention concepts.
